# Rotadent Contour: A "Do the Math" Product Launch

**Role:** Creative Director (design lead), Zila · **When:** 2013 · **Scope:** Brand identity, packaging, website, advertising, and the full launch campaign for a relaunched electric toothbrush

![The Rotadent Contour packaging and toothbrush — a sleek silver, white, and blue electric brush beside its retail canister.](https://adrian3.com/imgs/case-studies/images/Rotadent-Launch/01-packaging.jpg)

## Introduction

Rotadent isn't a drugstore toothbrush. It's a professional, dentist-sold electric brush with a 25-year history and a genuine cult following among the hygienists and patients who swear by it. In 2013, Zila completely redesigned it and relaunched it as **Rotadent Contour** — and as Creative Director I led the launch design across every touchpoint. The whole campaign hung on a single, simple idea: **"Do the Math."**

## 1. A Cult Brand, Reborn

### Honoring 25 years while signaling something new

The challenge with relaunching a beloved product is respecting its loyalists while convincing the market it's genuinely new. The refreshed Rotadent Contour identity kept the trusted name and a clean, clinical blue palette — credible to dental professionals — while the new brush-head mark and modern wordmark signaled that this was a real redesign, not a reskin.

![The Rotadent Contour logo — a blue wordmark beneath a stylized rotating brush-head mark.](https://adrian3.com/imgs/case-studies/images/Rotadent-Launch/02-logo.jpg)

## 2. The Idea: "Do the Math"

### Let the numbers be the hero

Most toothbrush marketing is interchangeable. Rotadent's advantage, though, was genuinely quantifiable — so instead of vague claims, we made the *numbers* the campaign. Rotadent Contour is the only brush that can claim **360° rotation, over 4,500 micro-access filaments (4,577, to be exact), and 1,350 revolutions per minute** — removing 92% of plaque in the first minute. "Do the Math" turned those specs into the through-line that connected every piece of the launch, from packaging to the website.

![A Rotadent Contour sales brochure spread, with the campaign's signature statistics — 4,577 micro-access filaments, 1,350 revolutions per minute, 360° rotation, 92% of plaque gone in the first minute — set as bold typography.](https://adrian3.com/imgs/case-studies/images/Rotadent-Launch/03-brochure.jpg)

## 3. A Full-Funnel Launch

### One idea, every touchpoint

A product relaunch lives or dies on consistency, so Zila's in-house marketing team produced the entire campaign as one coherent system — and I led the design across it, working with launch partners including Moxie Sozo, Definite Productions, Cambon Studios, and Syncroness.

The **website** made "the numbers add up" literally, leading with *"92% of plaque gone in the first minute"* and a clean path to purchase.

![The Rotadent.com website — "92% of plaque gone in the first minute / The numbers just add up," with product pages and a numbers-pattern background.](https://adrian3.com/imgs/case-studies/images/Rotadent-Launch/04-website.jpg)

**Advertising** ran in dental trade press, reframing the brush as more than a toothbrush — *"Think this is just another toothbrush? Think again."*

![A Rotadent Contour magazine ad — "Think this is just another toothbrush? Think again." — with a dramatic close-up of the brush filaments.](https://adrian3.com/imgs/case-studies/images/Rotadent-Launch/05-ad.jpg)

A **product video with 3D animation** showed what words couldn't — the 360° rotation and the micro-access filaments reaching where ordinary bristles can't, around braces, implants, and the gumline.

![Frames from the Rotadent Contour launch video — clinician presenters intercut with 3D animation of the brush head, 4,577 filaments, and 360° rotation.](https://adrian3.com/imgs/case-studies/images/Rotadent-Launch/06-video.jpg)

Because Rotadent is sold *through* dental practices, the launch also had to equip the people selling it. We produced a **training manual** to help reps and practices sell the new Contour, and **launch kits, direct mail, shirts, and tradeshow materials** that carried the "Do the Math" numbers all the way to the booth.

![The "Meet Contour" sales training manual — "Your guide to selling the new Rotadent" — open to a "Prepare Yourself" spread of the campaign statistics.](https://adrian3.com/imgs/case-studies/images/Rotadent-Launch/07-training-manual.jpg)

![Rotadent Contour launch kit and direct-mail pieces — a branded bag and "Meet Contour" mailers on the numbers-pattern background.](https://adrian3.com/imgs/case-studies/images/Rotadent-Launch/08-launch-kit.jpg)

## Conclusion

Relaunching a 25-year-old product to a loyal professional audience is a balancing act: honor what people love, prove what's new. "Do the Math" threaded that needle by letting Rotadent Contour's own measurable advantages carry the story — one idea expressed consistently across identity, packaging, web, advertising, video, sales tools, and the tradeshow floor. It's the kind of end-to-end launch where the design is only as strong as its weakest touchpoint, so every touchpoint had to add up.
