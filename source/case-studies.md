# Design

Over the course of my career I've worn many different hats. From production designer to manager to creative director, I'm comfortable contributing deep in the weeds, mentoring emerging talent, and giving high-level strategic guidance.

As a result, I have a portfolio of work that's hard to contain on one screen. What to do? Like anything I do, I view this challenge as a user experience question: how do I present the information so that you, my "user," can find what you're looking for? With that in mind, pick a topic that interests you and explore the artifacts of my career.

Across all these industries and disciplines one thing is consistent. I bring ideas to every project with a low-ego, "anything's possible" mentality. I care deeply about the people I serve and the problems I'm helping solve. I balance meticulous attention to detail with the ability to move fast and iterate. I'm intentional in my approach and committed to design principles and best practices. And I've built relationships with people that cross corporate hierarchies, different industries, and a wide range of personalities and professions.
