Hi, I’m Ade.

I’m a creator from Colorado who writes books, letters, and essays. I document my art experiments on Instagram. Experiments you ask? I make things like enormous cameras made of coffee stirrers. And I’ve sent hundreds of art postcards to my friends. I also enjoy reading good books, and running long distances. Thanks for checking out my website, let’s follow each other on the socials.

I write a newsletter called Made by Ade where you can get a taste of my twisted blend of technology critique, storytelling, and ideas for maintaining your creative energy. I want to add you to my distribution list.
